# Upsell: from registration to relationship

Manager direction (Sandeep): we build the events platform, and we also propose a marketing
campaign layer that uses the registration data to send PEIs, drive My Memorial Hermann app downloads
and bring people into the Memorial Hermann network. This is the value proposition.

> **PEI = patient engagement initiatives** (confirmed by Surya, 2026-10-15): the engagement
> programs run from registration data (follow-up, next class, app adoption, find a doctor,
> demand re-engagement).

## Value statement

Every registration tells Memorial Hermann what a person cares about. Use it to bring them back and
into the network. Consent-based: messages only to people who opt in at registration.

- 1 profile: every registrant, caregiver and provider in one Salesforce record
- 4 PEIs: patient engagement initiatives after every class
- 1 tap: app download link at confirmation, check-in and follow-up
- Closed loop: from class attended to visit booked, measured end to end

## Journey (Marketing Cloud)

Entry: registered for a class or event, then confirmation email with calendar invite and QR, then SMS
reminder 24 hours before, then a decision on attendance (from QR check-in).

- Attended: PEI 1 thank you + 2-question survey (same day), PEI 2 recommended next class, PEI 3 get
  the My Memorial Hermann app (SMS + email), PEI 4 find a doctor (OB, pediatrician, primary care).
  Exit when a visit is booked or the app is downloaded.
- No-show: "Sorry we missed you" with one-click rebook.

## Campaign examples

| After this class | Follow-up PEIs | Network outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Labor and delivery prep | Breastfeeding and newborn classes, choose a pediatrician | Delivery at MH, new pediatric patients |
| Maternity hospital tour | Pre-register for delivery, prenatal class bundle | Delivery booked at MH |
| CPR and first aid | Heart health seminar, free screening invitation | Primary care and cardiology visits |
| Support groups and seminars | Education series, specialist-led events | Ongoing specialty care relationships |
| Provider conference | Next CME events, referral resources | Stronger referral network |

## App download touchpoints

1. Confirmation email: app link beside the calendar invite and QR code
2. Check-in: QR at the door and on room signage
3. SMS reminder: manage or cancel the class in the app
4. Post-class PEIs: find a doctor, book a visit, see results in the app

Tracked links show which classes and service lines drive downloads and booked visits.

## KPIs (baselines set in discovery; no numbers promised)

Conversion, attendance (no-shows), PEI engagement, app downloads from event links, visits booked
after events, revenue.

## Roadmap

1. Classes and events (weeks 1-14)
2. Conferences (weeks 15-24)
3. Engagement (from Phase 1 go-live): PEI journeys by service line, app campaigns, find-a-doctor nurture
4. Intelligence (week 25+): Agentforce assistant, recommendations, class-to-visit attribution
