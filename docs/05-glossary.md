# Glossary

- **Classes:** small scheduled sessions for the public, usually part of a day: hospital or maternity
  tours, CPR, labor and delivery prep, breastfeeding, diabetes education, support groups. Held at
  hospitals, clinics, community sites or online (Zoom). Free or a small fee.
- **Events:** broader community activities such as health fairs, free screenings and heart health
  seminars.
- **Event management:** running events end to end: listing, registration, payment, reminders,
  check-in, attendance and follow-up. Not the same as a marketing campaign; a campaign promotes the
  event.
- **Conferences:** large one- or multi-day events, mostly for providers and clinicians (medical
  education). MH runs one or two a year, today on Certain.
- **Sessions:** individual talks or workshops inside a conference, each with a time, room, speaker
  and capacity. **Tracks** group related sessions (for example Cardiology).
- **CME:** continuing medical education credits that providers earn by attending sessions.
- **PEI:** post-event interaction (our working definition; confirm with Sandeep). An automated
  follow-up after a class or event.
- **Registrant types:** consumers (may or may not be patients), caregivers, providers.
- **Hosted checkout:** the payment gateway's own payment page, so card data never touches Salesforce.
- **My Memorial Hermann:** MH's Epic MyChart-based patient app.
