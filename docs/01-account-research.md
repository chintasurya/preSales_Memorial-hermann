# Account research: Memorial Hermann Health System

Last updated: 2026-10-15. Public sources only unless marked "client". Treat anything not marked
"client" as background, not confirmed fact.

## Organization

- Largest not-for-profit health system in southeast Texas (Houston). Roughly 15 to 17 hospitals and
  about 250 care sites (sources differ).
- Similar to Ascension in shape, but smaller.

## Vendors

Known: Perficient (CRM, historical), Sitecore, Vizient, Epic, Qualtrics, Certain, Chase,
Authorize.net. Historical: XactiMed (claims), WorkflowOne (print). No public evidence of offshore IT
vendors (Cognizant, Infosys, Wipro, TCS, Accenture, Optum); ask directly.

## Compliance history (background)

- 2017: $2.4M HHS OCR settlement for naming a patient in a press release.
- Separate $240K OCR right-of-access settlement.
- Unverified third-party report of a breach at vendor Advent Health Partners.
- Piedmont Healthcare (peer example): Meta Pixel class action over patient data on website and
  portal, dismissed Aug 2024 for lack of damages. Relevant as a caution for tracking on registration
  pages. Client asked us to leave consent / HIPAA out of discovery questions for now.
